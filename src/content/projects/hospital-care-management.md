My final degree project: a web app that helps hospital staff keep track of patients and the people caring for them. Doctors and administrators can look up a patient's records, see who's treating them, and update their care in one place instead of juggling several separate systems.

I built the frontend in Angular and the backend in Spring Boot, with MariaDB and MongoDB handling the data, and packaged the whole thing with Docker so it's easy to run anywhere.
