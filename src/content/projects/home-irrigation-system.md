An ESP32-based system designed to water the garden automatically instead of doing it by hand. Sensors report soil and weather conditions over MQTT, a FastAPI backend processes that data, and an Angular dashboard lets me check on everything and adjust it remotely.

It's not finished yet. I've paused it for now, but I'd like to pick it back up.
