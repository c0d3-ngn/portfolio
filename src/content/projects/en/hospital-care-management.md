My final degree project: a full stack web app that gives hospital staff a single place to manage patients and their care. Doctors and administrators can look up a patient's records, see who is treating them and update their care, instead of juggling separate systems.

I built it end to end: an Angular frontend, a Spring Boot backend, MariaDB and MongoDB for the data, and Docker to package everything so it runs anywhere.
